The first archive centers the Florida Project reunion and the 9th Ward community. It establishes the NOLA EDITION system: numbered releases, place-based storytelling and garments designed to function as records.

01 · The source

Florida P is not being used as decoration. Archive 001 starts with the neighborhood itself: the Florida Projects, the streets around them and the people whose memories give the place meaning.

The front designs approach that history from two directions. Design 01 is more celebratory and graphic. Design 02 is quieter and more archival.

Two fronts.
One back story.

Both designs connect to the same Florida Projects back panel, keeping the edition visually consistent while letting the front artwork tell the story in different ways.
